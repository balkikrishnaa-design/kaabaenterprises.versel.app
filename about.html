About Kaaba Furniture, a unit of Kaaba Enterprises

Furniture that starts as a tree, not a flat-pack box.

Kaaba Furniture, a unit of Kaaba Enterprises, is a solid wood furniture manufacturer based in Sangliyandapuram, Trichy. We build beds, dining sets, tables and almirahs the way they were built before veneer and particle board became the default — solid wood, cut and carved to the order in front of us.

Our History

A Trichy-based furniture manufacturer, hand-building beds, dining sets, tables and almirahs for homes across the region.

Our Materials

Primarily solid teak — including Ghana teak — along with mahogany. No plywood core, no veneer wrap.

Where We Deliver

We regularly serve customers within about 150 km of our Sangliyandapuram workshop, covering Trichy and the surrounding region.

Our Materials

Plywood bends. Solid wood doesn't have to.

A lot of "teak furniture" sold today is a plywood or MDF frame wrapped in a thin teak veneer — it looks right in a showroom photo but doesn't take re-polishing, re-carving, or decades of Trichy humidity well.

Every panel that leaves our workshop is solid wood, end to end — mainly teak, including Ghana teak, with mahogany available for certain pieces. It costs more to cut and takes longer to carve, but it's also the only kind of furniture you can sand, re-stain, and pass down instead of replacing.

How a piece gets built

Four stages, from rough timber to a finished piece in your room.

01 — Selection

Choosing the timber

Teak is sorted for grain and density before it's allowed near a saw — the wood decides what it can become, not the order sheet.

02 — Joinery

Cutting the frame

Bed posts, table legs and door frames are cut and joined to your size, not trimmed down from a standard mould.

03 — Carving

Hand-carved detailing

Scrollwork, lattice and panel carving is done by hand, piece by piece — it's the slowest stage, and the one we don't shortcut.

04 — Finishing

Polish and inspection

A final sanding and polish pass, then a check against the original order before it leaves the workshop.
